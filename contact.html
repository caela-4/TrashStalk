In Participation in Proper Waste Management, Environmental Awareness, and Community Participation, I Will...

Practice Proper Waste Segregation

I will separate biodegradable, non-biodegradable, recyclable, and residual waste and dispose of them in their designated bins.

Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle

I will minimize the trash I produce by avoiding unnecessary packaging, reusing materials, and recycling items that can still be used.

Keep My Surroundings Clean

I will dispose of my trash properly and avoid leaving garbage in streets, classrooms, and other public places.

Promote Environmental Awareness

I will encourage my family, friends, and classmates to practice proper waste segregation and responsible trash disposal.

Be a Responsible Member of the Community

I will follow proper waste disposal rules and participate in community clean-up activities to help maintain clean surroundings.

Take Accountability for My Actions

I will take responsibility for the trash I produce by disposing of it properly and avoiding actions that contribute to littering.
