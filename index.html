What is SDG 11?

SDG 11, also known as the “Sustainable Cities and Communities” Development Goal, is a goal created by the United Nations, aiming to transform cities into safer, more sustainable habitats by reducing urban hazards, optimizing resource efficiency, and ensuring resilient infrastructure for every resident.

As time goes by, the Philippines' dire need of assistance when it comes to creating thriving, livable cities increases. And because of that, we, Group 5, have developed a plan that can effectively help in that area, particularly towards garbage management and the cleaning of our streets. First, let us identify the problem at hand.
