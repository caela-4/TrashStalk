The Problem

Understanding Improper Waste Management and Its Effects on Flooding

UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

Improper waste management is a serious environmental problem in the Philippines, particularly in highly populated communities. Improperly disposed of garbage, overflowing trash bins, illegal dumping, and uncollected waste contribute to the accumulation of solid waste in streets, canals, rivers, and drainage systems.

One of the major consequences of this problem is the obstruction of waterways. When garbage accumulates in drainage systems, it prevents rainwater from flowing freely, which can worsen flooding during heavy rainfall and typhoons.

Flooding can affect various aspects of people's lives. It can damage homes and public infrastructure, disrupt transportation, negatively affect businesses, contaminate water sources, and expose communities to health and safety risks.

Furthermore, the continuous accumulation of waste contributes to water pollution, unpleasant surroundings, and environmental degradation. These conditions make communities more vulnerable to the effects of heavy rainfall and other environmental hazards.

DID YOU KNOW?

Facts and Information

01. Improper Waste Disposal and Flooding

According to the Department of Environment and Natural Resources – National Capital Region (DENR-NCR, 2025), improper waste disposal remains a major cause of clogged waterways, worsening flooding during periods of heavy rainfall.

02. Waste Accumulation in Metro Manila Waterways

According to the Philippine Information Agency (2025), more than 872,000 sacks of solid waste and debris were removed from critical waterways in Metro Manila during the first half of 2025 to help reduce flooding in vulnerable communities.

03. Flooding and Community Safety

According to the United Nations (n.d.), Sustainable Development Goal 11, Target 11.5, emphasizes the importance of reducing the number of people affected by disasters, including water-related disasters, and minimizing damage to communities and infrastructure.

04. Environmental Impact of Improper Waste Management

According to the United Nations (n.d.), Sustainable Development Goal 11, Target 11.6, highlights the importance of reducing the environmental impact of cities, particularly through proper municipal waste management and improved environmental conditions.
